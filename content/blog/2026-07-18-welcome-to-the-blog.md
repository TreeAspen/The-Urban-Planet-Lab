This is where we'll share longer-form writing — research reflections, methods deep-dives, and stories from the field — separate from the short updates on the [News](/news) page.

## What goes here

- Essays on our research directions
- Behind-the-scenes looks at fieldwork and data collection
- Longer write-ups that don't fit a short News post

Each post can either be a **long-form article** like this one, with its own page, or a **quick update card** that behaves like a News item — pick whichever fits when you publish from the admin.
